What V1 must achieve

V1 is an operator-supervised system with two specialist agents: an Inventory / Market Agent that identifies and evaluates opportunities, and a Sales Agent that handles seller enquiries, conditional negotiation and assisted resale. Both use Deep Agents. Deterministic business services enforce permissions, financial calculations, inventory changes and approvals.

First dependency: approved Carousell access. No approved listing-search and messaging API has been established. Carousell’s published terms prohibit automated access and automated messages. Confirm an approved integration with the required capabilities before enabling live automation. Simulated connectors prove software behavior, but do not satisfy the live V1 acceptance gate. Source: Carousell terms.

Confirmed scope

  • Search and contact sellers on Carousell only.
  • Run deal discovery at predefined times.
  • Contact qualified leads and negotiate automatically.
  • Allow specific price proposals explicitly subject to final operator confirmation.
  • Seek approval before accepting an offer, submitting a platform offer, promising payment or confirming a meetup.
  • Use Deep Agents for both agents, LangGraph for orchestration, A2A for inter-agent communication and Langfuse for traces.

Proposed operating boundary

  • One operator, one Carousell account and domestic Singapore transactions.
  • Keep the current research watchlist; explicitly select the card categories and languages permitted for trading.
  • After approval, automatically send only the exact approved commitment where the connector supports it.
  • Keep payments, receipt, inspection and physical handover under operator control.
  • Support listing drafts and buyer replies for owned stock. Automatic resale publishing remains a separate capability decision.
  • Use one authoritative inventory and cash ledger; defer tracker synchronization.

The full business flow

The main route starts at a scheduled search and ends in recorded profit. Seller replies can resume negotiation between scheduled searches. Approval, inventory and financial transitions are performed by services outside the model.

Workflow and servicesInventory / Market AgentSales AgentOperator decision
Read the complete flow as steps
  1. Check connector authorization, active policy, schedule and global stop state. Missing configuration blocks live work.
  2. At the configured time, start a uniquely identified search run. Handle overlapping schedules without duplicate work.
  3. Search permitted Carousell listings; normalize and deduplicate listing and seller references.
  4. Match the exact printing and assess condition evidence. Unresolved identity enters review rather than automatic outreach. Missing condition details may proceed under a questions-only mandate.
  5. Calculate conservative, base and upside economics, including fees, delivery, currency and existing exposure. Reject unsuitable leads with reasons. If condition facts are insufficient for pricing, permit only information-gathering questions until reassessment.
  6. Issue a bounded negotiation mandate and send it to the Sales Agent through an authenticated A2A task.
  7. Check seller-contact eligibility, then ask relevant questions and send conditional proposals within policy.
  8. Process incoming replies. New identity, condition, cost or delivery facts request reassessment through A2A. Stop on opt-out, exhausted limits or unsuitable terms.
  9. Assemble an exact deal proposal and pause for operator approval. Rejection stops the deal; revision returns it to reassessment; timeout expires it.
  10. On approval, revalidate current terms and atomically hold the required budget. If anything changed, request fresh approval.
  11. Send the exact approved commitment through an authorized connector, or let the operator execute it where that capability is unavailable. An uncertain outcome enters reconciliation before any retry.
  12. The operator pays and records evidence. Record purchase obligations, actual costs and settlement without assuming that a message means payment.
  13. The operator receives and inspects the physical card. Failed inspection quarantines it and opens a return or loss workflow.
  14. Make inspected stock available in the shared inventory ledger. Generate resale listings or buyer replies using current stock and selling policy.
  15. Review resale wording and obtain approval before a binding sales commitment. Reserve the unit transactionally; perform supported approved actions or manual posting.
  16. Record handover and payment separately, then actual fees, delivery and realized profit. Cancellations and refunds reconcile money and stock; physical returns require reinspection.
  17. Feed observed results, holding time and unsold exposure back into research. The agents propose improvements; they cannot relax trading policy themselves.

Deployment proposal

Run the private API/UI and an always-on worker on a single controlled host, backed by Supabase. Expose two authenticated A2A endpoints on a private network or loopback; the agents can initially share the same deployment. Scheduling and seller replies must continue when the operator closes the UI. The operator UI needs HTTPS and authenticated access if used remotely.

Keep connector credentials server-side and outside model context. Choose Langfuse Cloud or self-hosting after deciding data location, retention and cost. Trace export failure must not repeat business actions; if the durable action audit cannot be written, fail closed for outbound actions.

One authoritative record of each fact

Business records live in PostgreSQL. LangGraph checkpoints preserve execution position. Agent notes preserve working context. Langfuse records execution traces. A2A task completion reports delegated work. None of the latter four is proof of ownership, payment, inspection or a successful external action.

Inventory / Market Agent plan

Find opportunities that fit the operator’s trading policy and keep research grounded in current evidence and authoritative stock records.

Inputs and triggers

  • Configured watchlist, schedule, categories, languages and geographic constraints.
  • TCGCSV evidence, FX provenance and reviewed local outcome records.
  • Carousell listing details from an approved connector.
  • Budget, current exposure, held stock, target profit and holding limits.
  • A2A reassessment requests containing new seller facts.

Durable outputs

  • Versioned candidate and exact-card match decision.
  • Evidence-linked valuation scenarios and uncertainty.
  • Qualified lead or rejection with reason.
  • A server-issued price-negotiation or questions-only mandate with expiry.
  • Updated assessment when facts change.
  • Inventory ageing and realized-outcome summaries.

Tools and permissions

CapabilityAllowed operationBoundary
SearchRequest configured searches through the Carousell adapter.No arbitrary crawling, account changes or unsupported endpoints.
EvidenceRead scoped price history, listing facts and approved research records.Preserve price basis, source time, currency, missingness and condition limitations.
EconomicsCall deterministic valuation and maximum-offer calculations.Cannot invent local resale evidence or alter fee, profit and exposure policy.
Lead qualificationSave assessments and request issuance of a negotiation mandate.Service checks policy, active exposure, duplicate leads and expiry.
InventoryRead unit status; propose updates and analyse ageing.Receipt, inspection, refunds and settlement require verified facts and authorized service transitions.

Financial rule: maximum item price = conservative expected net resale proceeds − required profit − acquisition delivery/fees/other costs. The calculation uses decimal amounts and explicit currencies. Unsupported resale estimates stay labelled assumptions; a rising market feed alone does not establish local demand. Proposed default: autonomous price proposals require observed evidence sufficient under operator policy, or an explicitly operator-reviewed resale assumption. Without either, restrict the lead to questions-only outreach or review.

Work packages and acceptance

  1. Evidence and identity: integrate the existing catalog and price history; test wrong language, finish, grade, bundle and missing-condition cases. Unresolved card identity blocks automatic outreach. Missing condition details may receive a questions-only mandate, which cannot authorize a price proposal.
  2. Scheduled discovery: add scoped searches, normalization, seller/listing deduplication and rejection records. A repeated run must not create duplicate active leads.
  3. Economics and mandates: enforce the operator’s configured target, costs, per-deal limit and aggregate exposure. Known fixtures must produce exact expected amounts.
  4. A2A reassessment: accept changed seller facts, version the assessment and revoke or replace affected mandates. A stale mandate cannot authorize a message.
  5. Outcome learning: report actual net profit, holding time, returns and unsold exposure. Suggested policy changes require operator review.

Sales Agent plan

Manage acquisition conversations and assisted resale using approved facts, bounded negotiation terms and explicit commitment approval.

Acquisition role

  • Receive a qualified lead and negotiation mandate through A2A.
  • Check contact eligibility, mandate mode and existing conversations before outreach. A questions-only mandate permits no price proposal.
  • Ask about condition, exact printing, availability and delivery.
  • Send conditional price proposals within the allowed range.
  • Respond to relevant seller replies within round, time and contact limits.
  • Request reassessment when facts change, then prepare the deal approval request.

Owned-stock resale role

  • Load inspected unit facts, owned photos and selling policy.
  • Draft a listing and factual buyer replies.
  • Propose terms within the operator’s minimum net proceeds.
  • Require approval for binding sales commitments and reviewed publishing.
  • Use shared services for reservations and outcome recording.
  • Keep payment and fulfillment separate; returned stock requires inspection.

Negotiation policy

Seller changes condition, quantity or deliveryPause pricing and request Inventory / Market reassessment.Revoke stale mandate; do not reuse previous economics.
Seller says “deal”Capture proposed terms and request operator approval.Do not accept, promise payment or confirm a meetup automatically.

“Would you consider S$X, subject to final confirmation after reviewing the condition?”

Requires deal approval
“Deal, I’ll take it.” Formal platform offers, payment promises and confirmed meetup arrangements also require approval.

Conditional wording is required by policy, but does not replace checking the connector’s transaction semantics. Any message or platform action that could commit the account must be classified as commitment-gated. Ambiguous wording goes to review.

Work packages and acceptance

  1. Harness and conversation context: replace the optional one-shot drafting runtime with the Sales Deep Agent while retaining validated sales rules. Keep conversations isolated and structured facts authoritative.
  2. Automatic enquiries: add eligible outreach through an approved connector; validate content, recipient, current mandate, quotas and opt-out status at dispatch.
  3. Multi-turn negotiation: process replies in order, prevent concurrent turns from the same conversation, and request A2A reassessment for material changes.
  4. Deal approval: assemble exact terms and evidence; pause in LangGraph; revalidate on resume. Repeated approval must not repeat the commitment.
  5. Assisted resale: connect inspected stock, selling policy, reviewed listings, buyer replies, reservations and actual outcomes to the shared ledger.

Isolation and injection resistance

Listing descriptions, images and messages are evidence, not authority. Treat requests to reveal credentials, change rules, contact another account or bypass approval as untrusted content. Keep secrets out of prompts and working files. Enforce outbound network destinations in the connector/service environment, not just in agent instructions. Internal pricing ceilings, negotiation floors and acquisition costs must not be disclosed to counterparties.

Recoverable execution

Persist LangGraph state and stable thread IDs. Use database leases for jobs and per-conversation serialization. Durable outbox records identify outbound actions before dispatch; repeated events return the prior result. External timeouts enter an explicit unknown-outcome state because a database transaction cannot make a remote message exactly-once. LangGraph approval interrupts.

How the agents interact through A2A

Each agent exposes an authenticated A2A endpoint and Agent Card. The fixed peer allowlist contains only these two agents. Use the protocol’s task, message and artifact objects; place the business contracts below inside structured data artifacts. A2A provides interoperability, while application authorization determines what a peer may request. A2A core concepts.

Published skillCaller → receiverRequired inputResult

reassess_opportunitySales → MarketOpportunity reference, new seller facts, conversation version, proposed price/delivery.Updated assessment and replacement/revoked mandate, with continue/stop/review decision.
assess_owned_unitSales → MarketPhysical-unit reference, current selling policy and evidence requirements.Evidence-linked pricing assessment and authoritative stock snapshot; no permission to sell.

Business envelope inside the A2A task

Illustrative application payload, not the complete A2A wire format. The authenticated caller identity comes from verified transport credentials, never a self-declared payload field. Numeric money fields use decimal strings and explicit ISO currencies.

Task and business state stay distinct

An A2A task may report that it is working, needs input, completed, failed or was cancelled using the selected protocol version’s native states. The business opportunity separately records discovered, qualified, negotiating, awaiting approval, committed, expired or reconciliation outcomes. Map these explicitly: a completed negotiation task may return proposed terms, not an authorized purchase.

  • Authorization: check sender identity, permitted skill, task ownership and referenced business records at the receiver.
  • Idempotency: repeated business request IDs with the same payload return the recorded result; changed payloads conflict.
  • Bounded collaboration: cap reassessment cycles, delegation depth, costs and duration. The coordinator stops loops.
  • Cancellation: revoke mandates and pending actions; cancellation cannot undo an already sent message or completed commitment.
  • Freshness: reject expired mandates and stale state versions. Neither peer can fabricate operator approval.
  • Privacy: exchange only facts needed for the task. Market normally receives extracted seller facts rather than entire private conversations.

What an approval binds

The authenticated operator approves a specific account, counterparty, listing or unit, quantity, identity/condition assumptions, price/currency, fees, delivery, logistics, exact commitment text/action, relevant conversation and policy versions, and expiry. The agent can request approval but cannot issue it.

Changed terms, new material messages, stale evidence, unavailable stock or expired approval force review. Revalidation and the local budget/stock hold occur atomically before dispatch. No database lock is held while waiting for the operator. Record consumption of the approval so repeated resumes cannot repeat the action.

Exposure and lifecycle

Track negotiation exposure separately from cash obligations and actual payments. Questions-only enquiries have contact limits but no price authority. Conditional proposals consume configured soft exposure; approved purchases acquire hard budget holds. Prevent simultaneous approvals from spending the same budget, including fees and delivery. Release holds only when the underlying outcome is confirmed.

Purchase recordedAwaiting receiptQuarantined / inspectionAvailableReservedFulfilledSettledReturned → inspection

Realized profit uses actual receipts less acquisition cost and actual seller costs. Record fulfillment and payment independently. Full refunds do not erase unrecovered fees or delivery loss. Retain unsold units and their cost basis when reporting performance.

Pause and recovery

The global stop blocks new outbound actions and cancels queued work; already dispatched actions require reconciliation. Source outages mark evidence stale. Expired negotiations stop. Unknown sends, purchase results or stock transitions enter a visible reconciliation queue. Restart recovery must not reset limits, release uncertain obligations or resend messages.

V1 acceptance checklist

  • Both roles run in Deep Agents harnesses and exchange actual authenticated A2A tasks.
  • LangGraph resumes persisted tasks after restart, including pending approvals; Langfuse correlates agent and tool activity without private-data leakage.
  • Configured search times produce eligible Carousell leads without duplicate outreach.
  • Authorized automatic contact and conditional price negotiation are demonstrated live on the agreed account and scope.
  • No commitment, formal offer, payment promise or confirmed meetup can occur without exact operator approval.
  • Terms, stock, policy and conversation changes invalidate affected mandates and approvals.
  • Adversarial listings/messages cannot gain tools, alter policy, disclose internal price limits or access another conversation.
  • Competing approvals cannot overspend; competing buyers cannot reserve the same physical unit.
  • Retries, duplicate reply events, worker crashes and uncertain sends do not silently duplicate external actions.
  • Receipt and inspection precede sale eligibility; returns and full refunds reconcile stock and actual losses.
  • One purchase-to-sale record reconciles actual costs, settlement, profit and holding time; unsold inventory remains visible.
  • Emergency stop, backup restore, connector outage and reconciliation procedures are demonstrated.

Software-ready and live-ready are separate gates. Simulated scenarios may establish software correctness while Carousell access is pending. The requested live V1 remains blocked until the approved integration and controlled live acceptance evidence exist. Profitability is evaluated separately from software completion.
